Reposted a viral reel and got almost no reach? Instagram's originality rules are the reason.

Since 2024, Instagram stops recommending Reels from accounts that repost unoriginal content 10 or more times in 30 days, and shows the original clip instead when it finds identical copies. In April 2026 it extended the rule to photos and carousels.

Low-effort edits don't count: adding a watermark or changing the speed isn't enough, and a screenshot with the username for credit doesn't qualify. Your followers still see your posts, but you lose recommendations in feeds and Discover.

What works: your own voice-over, a reaction, or a meme where you add something that makes it better.
