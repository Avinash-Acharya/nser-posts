People tap your profile and leave? Instagram Search reads your name and bio. Here's the 10-minute fix.

Instagram has said its Search tries to "match what you type with relevant usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places." Its own tips: use a name that fits your content, and put keywords and your location in your bio.

Two more tools most creators don't use. Since April 2023 you can add up to 5 links to your bio (Mark Zuckerberg called it "a top request among creators"). Visitors see your first link plus "1 other", so put your best link first. And you can pin up to 3 posts or reels above your grid.

The 10-minute Instagram profile fix for Indian creators: name field = what you post + your city. Bio = who it's for. Best link first. Pin your 3 best posts.
