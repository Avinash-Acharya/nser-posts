Your Insights tab has dozens of numbers. Only 3 of them really tell you if a reel will reach more people.

Instagram head Adam Mosseri named three signals for reach: watch time, like rate (likes ÷ people reached) and send rate (sends ÷ people reached). Example: 1,000 people reached, 80 sends = an 8% send rate.

Instagram's newer insights also show skip rate: the share of viewers who scroll past in the first 3 seconds. And each reel now has a chart of how many people kept watching over time.

High skip rate? Change your first 3 seconds. Low send rate? Make something people want to send to a friend.
