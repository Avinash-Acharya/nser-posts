Think you're too old to start? She launched her YouTube channel after 50.

Nisha Madhulika started a blog of Indian vegetarian recipes in 2007, close to fifty. By 2011 she had written over 100 recipes, and that year she launched her YouTube channel. In 2017 she won the Top YouTube Cooking content creator award at the Social Media Summit & Awards. In 2020 her channel crossed 10 million subscribers.

She didn't chase trends. She made what she already knew: food. Write down three things you know, pick the one people ask you about most, and post once this week.
