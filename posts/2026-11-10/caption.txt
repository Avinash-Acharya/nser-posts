A brand sent you a free product and wants a reel, for no money? Here's how Indian creators turn freebies into paid deals.

Indian brands spent ₹3,600 crore on influencer marketing in 2024, and a WPP Media and Kantar report expects 25% growth in 2025. The same report says 83% of marketers struggle to find the right talent. Brands are looking.

But a free product is not "just a gift". ASCI's influencer rules say a material connection "isn't limited to monetary compensation": free products count, even if the brand didn't ask you to post. Label it (Ad, Collaboration or Free gift), upfront, not buried in hashtags.

The path from freebies to paid brand deals:
1. Say yes only to free products you'd buy anyway, with a small ask.
2. Make it your best work, labelled.
3. Screenshot the results.
4. Send this: "Loved working on this. Here's what the post did. For the next one, my rate for a reel is ₹___."

One report (Kofluence, 2025) puts reel rates for creators under 10,000 followers at ₹500 to ₹5,000.

Send this to a creator who only gets paid in freebies, and save it for your next brand DM.
