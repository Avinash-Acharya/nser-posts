Signed a brand deal contract without reading it? Check these 5 things first, especially number 3.

A contract is an agreement enforceable by law, so once you sign, you're bound by it.

1. Deliverables: how many reels and stories, and by when.
2. Payment: how much, and by what date.
3. Ownership: the creator is usually the first owner of the content. In India, assigning copyright has to be in writing and should name the work, the period and the territory. If the period isn't written, it's treated as five years; if the territory isn't written, it's limited to India.
4. Usage: can the brand run your reel as an ad, and for how long?
5. Exclusivity: for how long you can't work with other brands.

Read, ask, then sign. This is general information, not legal advice; for a big deal, ask a lawyer.
