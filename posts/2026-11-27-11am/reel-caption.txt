Your reel's audio got muted? Here's which music is actually safe to use.

Instagram's licensed music library is meant for personal, non-commercial use. That's why some business accounts and some types of posts don't get it, and some songs aren't available in every country.

The safe option: Meta's Sound Collection. Over 14,000 royalty-free songs and sounds you can use in Reels and Stories, even for ads. Making a paid reel for a brand? Use that, or your own original audio.

Also good to know: if rights agreements change, an old post's audio can get muted. The post still shows, and you can replace the muted track once. Shorter music clips are safer than full songs, and your video should always show something, not just play a track.
