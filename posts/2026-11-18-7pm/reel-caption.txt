Scared a new idea will flop in front of your followers? Instagram's trial reels let you test it on strangers first.

Turn on the "Trial" toggle before you share. The reel is shown to non-followers first. Your followers won't see it in Feed or on your grid, and only you can see it's a trial (a few may still find it through DMs or audio pages).

About 24 hours later you see views, likes, comments and shares. You can let Instagram auto-share it with your followers if it performs well within the first 72 hours, or share it with everyone yourself.

Try one new idea a week this way. Keep what works, learn from what doesn't.
