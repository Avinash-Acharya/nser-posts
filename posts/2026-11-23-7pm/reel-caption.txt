Your story gets views but zero replies? Add one sticker. Here's how polls and questions turn viewers into conversations.

The poll sticker: one question, two options. People vote and see live results, and you can see who voted and which option they picked in your viewers list. Stuck between two thumbnails? Let your audience decide.

The questions sticker: people can ask you anything, as many times as they want. Tap a question and your answer becomes a new story. When you share it, the asker's name and photo aren't shown.

Like the story itself, a poll disappears after 24 hours. So: add a poll today, a questions sticker tomorrow.
