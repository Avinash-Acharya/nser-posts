A brand sent you a "free" product. Under Indian tax rules, it may not be free at all.

A barter deal means you get a product instead of money, and the brand gets your reel. But a product you keep is a benefit that counts as business income. If a brand gives you benefits worth more than ₹20,000 in a financial year, it has to deduct 10% TDS on that value.

Example: you get a ₹30,000 phone, post the reel and keep it. No cash came in, but the value counts as income. The game changer: as per CBDT's FAQs, a product you return to the brand after the reel attracts no TDS.

Take barter only for things you'd buy anyway, send your rate card for everything else, and ask a CA before accepting expensive items. This is general info, not tax advice.
