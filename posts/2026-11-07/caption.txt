You don't need a big city to grow. A family from a Tamil Nadu village built the first Tamil channel to 1 crore subscribers.

Village Cooking Channel started in April 2018 in Pudukkottai. A former caterer, Periyathambi, and his grandchildren cook outdoors, in the fields and along the river banks. By January 2021 they had around 7 million subscribers. By July 2021, they passed 1 crore, the first Tamil YouTube channel to do it.

And the audience is there. In 2025, rural India had about 548 million active internet users, more than half of India's 958 million, and it is growing nearly four times faster than urban India.
