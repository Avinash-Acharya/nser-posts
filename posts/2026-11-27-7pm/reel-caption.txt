Tired of cold-DMing brands? Instagram has a marketplace where brands search for creators.

Creator Marketplace connects brands and creators for branded content and partnership ads. Meta tested it in the US from 2022 and in February 2024 announced it for eight new markets, including India. Brands search for creators using creator and audience filters.

Once you join, you can pick your preferred brand partners, and those brands see you under "Interested creators". Brand messages land in a separate Partnership messages folder, and brands can see your posts, average reach and follower count.

How to join: switch to a professional account, open your professional dashboard, tap Branded content, then Join creator marketplace. Set up your portfolio, and always use the paid partnership label on brand deals. (If you don't see the option, it isn't available to your account yet.)
