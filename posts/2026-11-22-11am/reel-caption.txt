Got a free product and posted without an "Ad" label? In India, that breaks ASCI's rules, even if you never asked for it.

ASCI's influencer guidelines: any material connection with a brand must be disclosed. Money, gifts, trips, and free products "with or without any conditions attached including those received unsolicited".

Use a clear label: Ad, Advertisement, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Free gift or Affiliate.

Where it goes matters:
1. Not buried in a group of hashtags, and not only in your bio, at the end, or behind "more".
2. If the caption isn't visible on a reel, put the label on the video itself. Videos up to 15 seconds: at least 3 seconds. 15 seconds to 2 minutes: a third of the video.
3. Live? Say it at the start and at the end.
4. Instagram's Paid Partnership tag is "in addition to" your own label, not instead of it.
