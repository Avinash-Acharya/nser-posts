DMed a brand and heard nothing back? Your message may be stuck in a pile they filter, not read.

Messages from accounts people don't follow land in "message requests", not the main inbox. Since late 2024, Instagram lets busy accounts sort and filter that pile by follower count, verified, business and creator account, so they can pick out the messages worth opening.

"Hi sir, collab?" gets skipped. Try this instead: the brand's name and one of their products in line one. One reel idea, just one. One number from your Insights, like your reels' average reach. Then one question: shall we talk? And use a professional (creator) account, since that's one of the filters.
