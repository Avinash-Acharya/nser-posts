One reel takes you hours. Why post it only once? Turn it into 5 posts.

Repurposing = same idea, new shape. Not copy-paste. Say your reel has 3 budget tips:
1. The reel itself.
2. The same tips as a carousel (one Instagram post can hold up to 20 photos and videos).
3. A YouTube Short (square or vertical videos up to 3 minutes count as Shorts).
4. A new hook with a new voiceover. Instagram lists "narrated with a new voiceover" as a significant change.
5. Your best line in a story, with a question for your audience.

The warning: accounts that post other people's content 10+ times in 30 days without adding real value get left out of recommendations. And just adding a border, watermark or subtitles doesn't make it original. Repurpose YOUR ideas, and add something new every time.
