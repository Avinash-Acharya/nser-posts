DMed a brand and got left on seen? You might be missing a media kit.

A media kit is your creator resume: one PDF that tells a brand who you are. There's no minimum follower count, and one or two pages is a good start.

What goes on it: a 2-3 line bio (who you are, what you make, who it's for), your numbers (followers, engagement rate, audience age, gender and location), past brand collabs with results, and a business email they can spot instantly. Rates are optional, but the scope (exactly what you'll make) should be clear.

Make one page today, and update your numbers before every pitch.
