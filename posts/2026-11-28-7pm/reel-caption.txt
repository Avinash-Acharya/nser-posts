Short reel or long reel? Here's what Instagram says, and what 2.7 million reels showed.

Instagram's rule: reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to people who don't follow you. Before January 2025 that limit was 90 seconds. You can now post reels up to 20 minutes, but anything over 3 minutes won't be recommended to new audiences.

Buffer studied 2.7 million reels (each account compared with itself):
- 90 seconds to 3 minutes: about 7% more reach than the account's usual, on average
- Under 90 seconds: length made almost no difference
- Over 3 minutes: about half the reach, but the highest engagement

So: to reach new people, stay under 3 minutes. Got long content? Cut it into a series, or keep the long version for your followers.
