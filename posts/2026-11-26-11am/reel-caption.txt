You show products in every video, but only the brand gets paid? YouTube Shopping lets you earn a cut in India.

The YouTube Shopping affiliate program came to India in October 2024, starting with Flipkart and Myntra. You tag products in your videos, Shorts and live streams. When a viewer taps the tag and buys on the retailer's site, you earn a commission set by the retailer.

Commission is paid through AdSense 60 to 120 days after the purchase, and returns reverse it. Sharing these affiliate links outside YouTube can get your affiliate features suspended.

To qualify you need to be in the YouTube Partner Program; music channels, Official Artist Channels and made-for-kids channels are not eligible. Then tag only products you actually use.
