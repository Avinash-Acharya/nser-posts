One reel, two audiences: that's what an Instagram Collab post does.

When you post, tap Invite collaborators. Once the other creator accepts, the post appears on both profile grids, goes out to both sets of followers, and shows both names at the top. Likes, comments and shares add up on the one post.

You can add up to five collaborators, it works for photos, carousels and reels, and there's no minimum follower count.

Pick three creators in your niche, send them one reel idea, and invite them when you post. Send this to the creator you want to collab with, and save it for your next post.
