Reel or carousel? If you guess every time, this is for you.

Instagram says most of what you see in Reels comes from accounts you don't follow. A 2026 study of more than 3 million posts backs it up: for accounts under 10,000 followers, reels reached nearly three times as many non-followers as carousels. Even above a million followers, reels still reached roughly twice as many.

So pick by your goal, not habit. Want new people? Make a reel. Teaching steps? Make a carousel. Great idea? Make both. Then check Insights: followers vs non-followers.
