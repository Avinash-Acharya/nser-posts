Still paying to edit your reels? Instagram has its own free editing app: Edits. Here's what it does.

Meta launched Edits in April 2025. It's free on the App Store and Google Play, and you sign in with your Instagram account.

What you get: a camera that records up to 10 minutes, a frame-accurate timeline, green screen and transitions. Share straight to Instagram, or export with no watermark and post anywhere.

The game changer: it gives feedback on things that can affect your reach, like skip rate. And the December 2025 update added more fonts for Indian languages, plus storyboards for your script, teleprompter and notes.
