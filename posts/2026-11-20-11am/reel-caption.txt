The stuff you already use every day can pay you. Here's how affiliate links work in India.

An affiliate link is your own special link to a product. When someone buys through it, you earn a fee. Amazon India's Associates program says "up to 10%", but each category has its own rate: 8% on beauty, 7.5% on bags, just 1% on mobile phones (rates as of Oct-Nov 2026, they can change).

The game changer: items added to the cart within 24 hours of a click on your link can count too. The rule you can't skip: you must clearly say it's an affiliate link. Payout comes once you've earned at least ₹1,000.

Only link what you really use, say where the link is, and disclose it every time. Instagram creators: check whether you need Amazon's separate Influencer Program.
