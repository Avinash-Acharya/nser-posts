New page, zero followers? Here's what to post in week one (and why "going viral" isn't the plan).

Your first 1,000 followers usually come from a few months of showing up, not one lucky reel. Buffer looked at 52 million+ posts from 200,000+ accounts: accounts posting 3-5 times a week grew followers about 2x faster than accounts posting once or twice.

Week one, keep it simple:
1. Add one or two keywords to your name field so people find you in search.
2. Post 3 reels a week. In Buffer's data, reels got 36% more reach than other post types.
3. Reply to every comment. Buffer found it lifts engagement by 21%.
Bonus: a trial reel goes to non-followers first, so you can test a hook without your followers seeing it.

Three times a week, every week, beats daily for two weeks and then vanishing. Not viral. Regular.
