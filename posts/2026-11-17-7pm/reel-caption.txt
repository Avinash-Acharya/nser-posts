"What's your rate?" If that DM makes you freeze, this is your 1-page fix.

A rate card is one page: what you charge for a reel, a story and a post. Kofluence's 2025 report says nano creators (1K-10K followers) earn about ₹500-5,000 per reel.

Price on views, not followers: average views ÷ 1,000 × ₹100-400 (higher for niches like finance and tech). 20,000 average views × ₹200 = ₹4,000 a reel.

If the brand wants to run your reel as an ad, charge for that separately. In the festive season, rate cards go up 15-30%. When a brand haggles, cut the deliverables, not your rate, and ask a new brand for half upfront.

Save this for the next time a brand asks your rate.
