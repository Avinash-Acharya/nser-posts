Zero followers? Brands can still pay you. It's called UGC.

UGC means you shoot a short, phone-made video and the brand buys it to use on its own page and ads. That's why they look at your video, not your follower count.

A 30-second unboxing goes for roughly ₹2,500-4,500 in India, and if the brand runs it as a paid ad, creators often charge 20-50% more.

Start today: pick 3 things at home, shoot an unboxing, a problem-to-fix and a review, put them on a free portfolio page and pitch D2C brands on LinkedIn.
