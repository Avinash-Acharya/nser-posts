Got over ₹20,000 of free products from one brand this year? 10% TDS applies. Here's how to claim it back.

India's Income Tax Department: if a business gives you benefits or perquisites (like free products) worth more than ₹20,000 in a financial year, tax is deducted at 10%. On the full value, not just the part above ₹20,000.

Example: one brand sends you ₹30,000 of products in a year → ₹3,000 TDS.

The game changer: the department's FAQ says a product you use for your content and then return is not treated as a benefit. Keep it, and TDS applies.

What to do:
1. Ask every brand for your Form 16A (the TDS certificate) and keep it safe.
2. File your ITR. That's how you get credit for the tax deducted.
