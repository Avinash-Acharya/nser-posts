Reels or Shorts? You don't have to pick. Here's how the money works on each.

Same vertical video, two different paydays. Meta says India is a leading country for Reels watch time on Instagram, so your audience and brand messages are there.

On YouTube Shorts, monetizing creators keep 45% of their allocated share of Shorts ad revenue. But from 1 Feb 2027, YouTube says you'll need at least 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days to earn that share.

So make one video and post it on both: Reels to build your audience and land brands, Shorts for view money.
