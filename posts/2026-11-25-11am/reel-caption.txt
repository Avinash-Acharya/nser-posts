Your reel doesn't reach every follower? One Instagram feature puts your message straight in their inbox.

A broadcast channel is a one-to-many chat on Instagram: only you send messages. You can share text, photos, videos, voice notes and polls. Followers can't reply in the chat, but they can react and vote in polls.

When you send your first message, your followers get a one-time notification to join. Anyone can find the channel and read it, but people who don't follow you are asked to follow before they join.

Try this: start the channel from your inbox, add the join sticker to your story, then post a poll asking what your next reel should be about.
