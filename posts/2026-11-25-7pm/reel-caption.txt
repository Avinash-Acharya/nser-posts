Every morning: "which reel do I make today?" Make the whole week's reels in one day instead.

Batching means shooting several reels in one sitting. Set the light and camera up once, then shoot back to back.

1. Write all your ideas in one list, each with its hook.
2. Change outfits between reels, so each one feels like a different day.
3. Edit them all together, in one style.

Then schedule them inside Instagram. With a professional account, you can schedule reels, posts and carousels up to 75 days ahead: tap Advanced settings, turn on "Schedule this post", and pick the date and time.

The rest of the week is for replying to comments and new ideas.
