The brand says "send the invoice". Here's exactly what goes on it.

If you're GST registered, Rule 46 of the CGST Rules lists what a tax invoice must have:
- Your name, address and GSTIN
- An invoice number: up to 16 characters, unique in each financial year
- The date
- The brand's name, address and GSTIN
- What you did, the service code, the taxable value, and the GST rate and amount
- For a brand in another state: the place of supply, with the state name

Sent after the work is done? The GST Council's invoice guide gives 30 days from the date of the service.

Simple habit: one template, numbers in one series (INV/26-27/001, 002...), send it on delivery day. Any GST doubt, ask a CA.
