Brand deal money coming in? Here's when GST registration kicks in for Indian creators.

If you supply services, you need GST registration once your yearly turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura). It's counted on an all-India basis, on your PAN, so brand deals, AdSense and foreign clients all add up.

Below the limit, you don't have to register even if your clients are in other states or abroad. Once registered, most creator services attract 18% GST. Export income like AdSense can be zero-rated if you file an LUT before your first export invoice of the year.

Track your total income every month, and talk to a CA when you get close to the limit. This is general info, not tax advice.
