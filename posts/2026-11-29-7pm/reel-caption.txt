Some fans watch every story you post. Here's how Instagram lets them pay you.

Close Friends: a private list only you can see or edit. Nobody is notified when you add or remove them, and people on it see a green ring on your story.

Subscriptions: fans pay you every month for exclusive reels, stories, lives and more, plus a badge. To be eligible you must be at least 18 with more than 10,000 followers, and you choose the monthly price. It has rolled out country by country, so check your professional dashboard.

Not at 10,000 yet? Use Close Friends as a test room: add your most active fans, post behind the scenes, and watch what they reply to most. Once you're eligible, that's your subscription content.
