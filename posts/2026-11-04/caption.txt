Nisha Madhulika started her YouTube channel after 50. In 2020, she crossed 10 million subscribers.

She began in 2007, in her late forties, with a blog of Indian vegetarian recipes. By 2011 she had written over 100 recipes, and that year she launched her YouTube channel. In 2017 she won the Top YouTube Cooking content creator award at the Social Media Summit & Awards. In 2020 she crossed 10 million subscribers and got YouTube's Diamond Play Button.

The lesson: you don't need to be young. Start with what you know, and keep going.
