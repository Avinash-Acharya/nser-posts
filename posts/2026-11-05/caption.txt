Reel or carousel? Reels reached nearly 3x more non-followers than carousels for small accounts.

Instagram says most reels you see come from accounts you don't follow. Your feed is mostly people you already follow. A 2026 study of more than 3 million posts backs this up: for accounts under 10,000 followers, reels reached 4.9% of followers per post, photos 3.5% and carousels 2.6%. And reels reached nearly three times as many non-followers as carousels.

The lesson: use reels to get found by new people. Use carousels to teach the people who already follow you. Got a good idea? Make both.
